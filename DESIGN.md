# 815live design: "Telephone Pole"

A gig flyer stapled to a pole outside the bar: black ink and poster pink with
a strip of yellow tape, on newsprint. Flush-left, square corners, heavy rules,
no soft cards. The site (`src/styles/global.css`) and the social images
(`scripts/social/`) both follow this file; change them together.

## Palette

Light is the default; the site switches to "black stock" for
`prefers-color-scheme: dark`. Social images always use light.

| Token | Light | Dark | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `--paper` | `#e4e2dd` | `#141414` | Background (newsprint) |
| `--ink` | `#141414` | `#ece9e3` | Text, heavy rules, borders, offset shadows |
| `--muted` | `#45423d` | `#b9b4ab` | Secondary text (venue · town, sources, dates) |
| `--accent` | `#b0124f` | `#ff6b9d` | Poster pink: category labels, "815", buttons |
| `--accent-text` | `#ffffff` | `#141414` | Text on pink |
| `--yellow` | `#ffd21a` | `#ffd21a` | Tape strips, ticket stub, today, skyline windows |
| `--on-yellow` | `#141414` | `#141414` | Text on yellow (always black) |
| `--hairline` | `#b4b0a8` | `#4a4740` | Quiet borders (empty calendar days only) |
| footer bg / text / muted | `#141414` / `#e4e2dd` / `#c9c5bd` | `#0a0a0a` / `#ece9e3` / `#b9b4ab` | Footer on black stock |

"Free" is a badge: yellow text on ink (light), ink text on yellow (dark).

Contrast, WCAG AA (normal text needs 4.5:1): every text pair above passes.
Lowest are pink on newsprint at 5.33 and white on pink at 6.90; ink on yellow
is 12.71. Don't put pink text on yellow, or muted text on ink.

## Fonts

Two families, both SIL OFL, self-hosted. No Google Fonts or other CDN.

| Family | Weights | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Anton** | 400 (only weight) | Headlines, show times, day tapes, nav, numerals, the 815 mark. Always UPPERCASE. |
| **Atkinson Hyperlegible** | 400, 700 | Everything else: act names in lists, venue, town, body copy, buttons. |

Files:

- Site: `public/fonts/anton-latin-400-normal.woff2`,
  `atkinson-hyperlegible-latin-{400,700}-normal.woff2` (`@font-face` at the
  top of `global.css`; Anton and Atkinson 400 are preloaded in `Base.astro`).
- Social images: satori can't read woff2, so the same fonts are committed as
  woff in `scripts/social/fonts/` (with their OFL licences). Load them from
  there; nothing is fetched.

Fallbacks: Anton → `'Arial Narrow', Impact, sans-serif`;
Atkinson → `system-ui, sans-serif`.

## Shapes and pieces

- **Rules, not cards.** 2px ink rules between shows; 6px rules open each home
  section and sit under the header. Square corners everywhere (radius 0).
- **Offset print shadow:** `3px 3px 0` ink on buttons, `5px 5px 0` on the
  facts box. No blurred shadows.
- **Tape strip:** yellow, Anton, slightly rotated: dates and day headings.
- **815 mark:** a yellow admission-ticket stub with a dashed tear line,
  "ADMIT ONE" and "815" in Anton (`src/components/Mark815.astro`; rotated −4°
  beside "LIVE"). A round Rockford postmark variant exists
  (`kind="postmark"`), unused.
- **Rockford skyline:** `src/components/RockfordSkyline.astro`, generated by
  `scripts/design/skyline.mjs`. viewBox 1200×240, river from y=178. Inks:
  buildings, trees and river `#141414`; far canopy and bridges `#b0124f`;
  windows, reflections and lamps `#ffd21a`; ripples `#e4e2dd`. On the home
  page it sits under the header; elsewhere it's a band above the footer.
- **Paper grain:** a tiny inline SVG noise tile at 9% alpha (site only).
- **Favicon:** `public/favicon.svg` (`scripts/design/favicon.mjs`).

| Part | Font | Size |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wordmark | ticket stub + Anton LIVE | 58 px |

Margins 64 px each side. Packing: everything at normal size, else tight,
else music on one tight image with other events in the room left (dropped
karaoke-first with a "+ N more at 815live.com" line), else music split over
images. Quiet nights (fewer than 3 music listings) are one "Tonight + coming
up" image; posts never say "no live music listed".
