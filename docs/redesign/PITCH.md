# Redesign: audit and three directions (2026-11-22)

Branch `redesign`. Visual only: no data, listing logic or URL changes.
Previews: `/design-preview/a`, `/b`, `/c` (noindex, same real listings in each;
deleted once a direction is applied). Screenshots in `previews/`, current site
in `before/`.

## What reads as "default AI site" today

From `src/styles/global.css`, `src/layouts/Base.astro`, `public/favicon.svg`
(no Tailwind in the project):

1. **Cream + terracotta.** `--bg: #f7f4ee` with `--accent: #c73a22`, the
   exact pairing, repeated in `theme-color` and the favicon.
2. **System font stack.** `system-ui, -apple-system, 'Segoe UI', Roboto`
   for body; the display face is whatever condensed font the OS has
   (Avenir Next Condensed, Roboto Condensed or Arial Narrow), so it looks
   different on every phone and like nothing in particular on any of them.
3. **Soft rounded boxes.** `--radius: 10px` on the tonight panel, fact boxes,
   day chips, calendar cells and buttons; 1px hairline borders; a 4px accent
   top-border on the tonight card (a very common template move).
4. **Pills everywhere.** `border-radius: 999px` on Submit, filters, chips and
   calendar counts.
5. **Tiny uppercase letter-spaced eyebrows.** `.category`, `.eyebrow`,
   `.also-heading`, `.dc-label` all use 0.75rem / 700 / uppercase / 0.06em
   tracking.
6. **Generic wordmark.** "815" in a rounded accent tab plus an "on-air" dot;
   the favicon is the same thing as a rounded square.
7. **One narrow centred column** (46rem) with nothing at the edges: no
   imagery, texture or sense of place anywhere on the site.
8. **Arrow links** ("See everything on the calendar →") and dashed dividers.

Not present (so nothing to remove): gradient hero, emoji icons, drop shadows.

Things worth keeping: the listing structure (time column + title + venue ·
city · cover), the day strip, the 44px social tap targets, the 46rem
reading width on desktop.

## A: Telephone Pole (gig poster / screenprint)

*A flyer stapled to a pole outside the bar: two inks and a yellow, pressed
hard into newsprint, loud enough to read from across the street.*

| Role | Hex |
| --- | --- |
| Newsprint (bg) | `#e4e2dd` |
| Ink (text, rules) | `#141414` |
| Ink, soft (muted) | `#45423d` |
| Poster pink (accent) | `#b0124f` |
| Tape yellow (highlight) | `#ffd21a` |

Type: **Anton** (headlines, times) + **Archivo** 400/600/800.
Layout: flush-left, no cards; heavy black rules between shows, nights tagged
with tilted yellow tape strips, the skyline as a linocut band under the
header, offset-print shadows on buttons, faint paper grain. Mark: ticket stub.
Light only as drawn; a dark mode would be "ink on black paper".

| Role | Hex |

| Letter board | `#f6efdc` |
| Board ink | `#1a1210` |
| Marquee red | `#a3122a` |
| Neon (accent on dark) | `#ff5468` |

Type: **Limelight** (headers, wordmark) + **Barlow Condensed** 600/700

Risk: three fonts, and the framed boards cost some width on a phone.

## C: Rock River Night

*Standing on the Jefferson Street bridge at 11 PM: dark water, sodium lights on
the ripples, and somewhere downtown a band is still playing.*

| Role | Hex |
| --- | --- |
| Night (bg) | `#0b1724` |
| Deep water (panels) | `#112236` |
| River (rules) | `#173a5c` |
| Sodium amber (accent) | `#ffb547` |
| Moonlight (secondary) | `#bfd3e6` |
| Text | `#e7eef6` |
| Muted | `#a3b6ca` |

Type: **Big Shoulders Display** 700/800/900 (headers, times; designed for
Chicago's civic signage) + **Atkinson Hyperlegible** 400/700 (body; built
for legibility, good on phones).
Layout: skyline and river across the top with the wordmark in the night sky;
open rows on deep blue with amber times; each night marked by a streetlight
dot. Mark: Rockford postmark ("Rockford · Illinois · Forest City").
Would need a daytime light variant for `prefers-color-scheme: light`.

## Shared pieces

- `src/components/RockfordSkyline.astro`: original flat SVG (generated by
  `scripts/design/skyline.mjs`): Rock River with ripples and reflected window
  light, an arched road bridge and a truss rail bridge, a downtown of stepped
  towers, a dome, a theater with a blade sign and marquee, a sawtooth-roof
  factory and smokestack, and Forest City trees along both banks. No traced
  photos or real logos. Coloured by CSS custom properties (`--sk-*`).
- `src/components/Mark815.astro`: 815 as ticket stub, bulb marquee or
  postmark.
- Fonts are self-hosted in `public/fonts/` (latin woff2, all OFL); no
  Google Fonts calls.

## Contrast (WCAG AA, normal text needs 4.5:1)

All text pairs used in the previews pass. Lowest: A pink on newsprint 5.33,
B free-green on letter board 5.75, B neon on house 6.14. C's lowest is
muted on deep water, 7.74.
